Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

Spendly collects the information you provide when you use the app, such as your name, email address, account credentials, and the expense details you enter (amount, category, date, and notes). We may also collect basic technical information needed to keep the service running, such as browser type or device information.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to create and manage your account, store and display your expense records, and help you review your spending over time. Your information is used only to provide the core features of Spendly and improve the reliability of the service.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application’s database and retained for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the service. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for hosting, infrastructure, or essential app functionality. These providers may process limited data on our behalf only as necessary to operate the service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the support channel provided with this application.
